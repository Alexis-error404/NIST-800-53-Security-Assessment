# Interview Guide

### Tell me about the project.
“I used a Windows/AD lab to practice a GRC assessment. I scoped the system, selected NIST 800-53 controls, defined evidence, assessed implementation, documented gaps, and created remediation and POA&M items.”

### Control vs evidence?
“A control is the safeguard or requirement. Evidence supports whether that control is designed and operating as expected—for example a configuration, log, access review, ticket, or approved procedure.”

### How did you assess implementation?
“I started with the control objective, identified appropriate evidence and examine/interview/test procedures, then documented whether the evidence supported implementation. I didn't assume that a configured setting automatically proved operating effectiveness.”

### What changes in production?
“I'd use the organization's real categorization, baseline/tailoring, defined parameters, control owners, inherited controls, production evidence, sampling requirements, formal assessment procedures, and authorization process.”

### Biggest lesson?
“Technical security and governance are connected but different. A technical setting becomes a sustainable control when ownership, evidence, review, exceptions, and remediation are repeatable and documented.”
